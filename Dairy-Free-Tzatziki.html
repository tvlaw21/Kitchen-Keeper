Dairy-free Tzatziki


This dairy-free tzatziki recipe is just as delicious as the traditional version! It's also naturally gluten-free, vegan, and low-carb.

⏰ Total cook time: 40 min

🍽 Makes 6 servings


Ingredients









Instructions

  1. Peel half of the cucumber lengthwise, leaving strips of peel on the other half. Grate or finely chop the cucumber, then squeeze out any remaining liquid from the cucumber.
  2. In a medium bowl, combine the dried cucumber, vegan yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, dill, salt, and pepper. Mix well until all the ingredients are evenly distributed.
  3. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate the tzatziki for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld. Serve chilled and enjoy.
